What to look for

    
Each line is a search term (a regular expression, case-insensitive). A plain word works.
       Pastes that contain a match are kept in the Matched folder.

    

    
  

  

    
Channels to watch

       Private invite links are not supported on purpose. Saving applies it straight away.
